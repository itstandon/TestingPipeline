### 5. TOP 5 CRITICAL GAPS

| Rank | Gap (short description) | Why it is critical |
|------|--------------------------|---------------------|

## QUICK ACTION RECOMMENDATION  

1. **Prioritise the missing functional flows** (registration, speciality management, appointment scheduling) by creating automated end‑to‑end tests that mirror the SRS‑derived cases.  
2. **Add security‑ and performance‑testing** to the code‑based suite (e.g., OWASP ZAP scans, load‑testing scripts).  
3. **Document the model‑level helpers** (the “undocumented features”) in an updated SRS or a separate technical specification to avoid future scope creep.  
4. **Map each SRS‑derived test to a concrete code path** (unit, integration, UI) so that the gap count can be reduced to zero.
