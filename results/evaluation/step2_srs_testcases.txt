## Virtual‑Clinic – System‑Level End‑to‑End Test Suite  

**Purpose** – Verify that the complete “Virtual Clinic” solution works correctly for all actors (Admin, Patient, Doctor, Lab‑In‑charge, Chemist) and satisfies the functional and non‑functional requirements described in the SRS (sections 2.1‑2.7).  
**Scope** – The test cases below exercise **whole‑system flows**, not unit‑level details. They cover the main business processes, error‑handling, security/privacy, performance, scalability and usability expectations.

> **Notation**  
> * **TC‑ID** – unique identifier (e.g., TC‑F‑01, TC‑N‑02)  
> * **Type** – **F** = Functional, **N** = Non‑functional, **S** = Security/Privacy, **U** = Usability.  
> * **Priority** – P1 (must), P2 (should), P3 (nice‑to‑have).  
> * **Traceability** – reference to SRS clause(s) that the test validates.
