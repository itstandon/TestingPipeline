**How to use this suite**

* **Execution order** – Start with the registration & admin setup cases (TC‑REG‑001 – TC‑REG‑004, TC‑ADM‑001 – TC‑ADM‑002) to provision data.  
* **Dependency mapping** – Each subsequent case lists the required pre‑conditions (e.g., a doctor must exist before a prescription can be created).  
* **Coverage traceability** – Every case references the relevant **Req‑**, **UC‑**, **BR‑** numbers, so you can map back to the SRS.  

These 30 test cases give a **full‑stack, end‑to‑end validation** of the Virtual Clinic system, addressing functional correctness, error handling, boundary conditions, security enforcement, performance under load, and integration of all major components (Admin → Patient → Doctor → Chemist → Lab → Reporting).
